💡 FIRE Tips

4% Rule: This rule suggests you can safely withdraw 4% of your portfolio annually in retirement without running out of money.

Savings Rate: The higher your savings rate, the faster you'll reach FIRE. Aim for 50%+ for aggressive FIRE timelines.

Investment Returns: Historical stock market returns average 7-10% annually, but past performance doesn't guarantee future results.

Inflation: Remember that your expenses will likely increase over time due to inflation. Factor this into your calculations.
