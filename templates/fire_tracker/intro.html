What is FIRE and why should you care?

FIRE is a lifestyle movement focused on extreme savings and investment that allows you to retire much earlier than traditional retirement age.

What is FIRE?

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early. It's a movement that challenges the traditional 40-year work cycle.

Financial Independence

Having enough money saved and invested to cover your living expenses without needing to work.

Retire Early

Freedom to stop working for money and pursue what truly matters to you, often in your 30s or 40s.

Freedom & Choice

The ultimate freedom to choose how you spend your time, energy, and life.

Why FIRE Matters

Traditional retirement planning often means working until 65+. FIRE offers a different path.

Traditional vs FIRE Approach

Traditional Retirement

Work 40+ years, retire at 65, hope you have enough saved

FIRE Approach

Save aggressively, invest wisely, retire in your 30s or 40s

Traditional: Limited Time

20-30 years of retirement, often with health limitations

FIRE: More Freedom

30-50 years of freedom to pursue your passions
